About TuncStudio

One developer, many small worlds.

TuncStudio is a one-person studio. I design, code, draw and support everything you find here myself. I like games you can play with one thumb on the bus, and apps that do one job well without asking for your life story. When you email me, I'm the one who reads it.

01Handcrafted

Hand-drawn pixel art, tuned levels and small details you notice on the second play.

02Easy to pick up

One-thumb controls and short sessions. Most of it works offline, too.

03Respects your privacy

No sign-up walls. Your data stays on your phone wherever it can.

04Fair to play

Free to download, with no pay-to-win and no tricks to keep you hooked.
