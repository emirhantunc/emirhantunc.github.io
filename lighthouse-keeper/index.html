Lighthouse Keeper

The old keeper vanished in the winter storms. Now the light is yours.

Every night, ships try to reach the harbor through the dark. Drag to swing your lighthouse beam: ships in the light sail home, ships left in the dark drift toward the rocks. Brave fog, storms, whirlpools, ghost ships and something beneath the waves, and uncover what happened to the keeper before you.
