Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Lighthouse Keeper ("the game") is a single-player game made by Emirhan Tunç. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

We (the developer) do not collect or store any personal information. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases, and you can play it without an internet connection.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between nights. When you are online, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and ad interactions to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On iPhone you can allow or deny tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. Playing offline shows no ads.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (nights completed, coins, upgrades, decorations, letters read and settings) is saved in a small file on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it, and you can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress.

Game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information (for example device model and crash or performance data) as described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to general-audience content (rated PG or lower).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
