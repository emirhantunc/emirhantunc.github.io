FAQ

How do I play?
Drag anywhere on the screen to swing the lighthouse beam. Ships inside the light sail safely to the harbor. Ships left in the dark slow down, then panic (a red "!") and can crash into rocks. Lose three ships and the night is lost; survive until dawn to win.

What does the foghorn do?
Once bought in the Workshop, tap the horn button during a night: for a moment every ship sails as if it were lit.

How do I get more letters?
Letters arrive with the blue mail sloop. Bring it safely into the harbor. If it sinks, the letter comes another night.

How do I drive away the sea serpent?
Shine your beam on it until it dives.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Not yet. Progress is stored only on your device.

How do I start over?
Settings (gear icon) → Reset progress.
