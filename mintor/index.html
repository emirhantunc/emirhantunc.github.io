Overview

Your whole month at a glance

Balance, income, spending and savings rate the moment you open the app. Your month starts when you say it does: pick payday or your card's statement day, and every total follows it.

Weekly limit

A limit that warns you in time

Set a weekly spending limit and Mintor tracks it against your real expenses. At 80% you get a heads-up, not at the end of the month when it's too late. A donut chart shows exactly where it went.

Quick entry

Add an expense in seconds

Amount, category, account, done. Mark it recurring and rent, salary and subscriptions post themselves every month.

Debts

Who owes who, settled

Track what you owe and what you're owed in one place. Record part payments, watch the balance come down, and get a flag when something is overdue.

Every Monday

Your week in numbers

A recap of what you spent, your top categories and your biggest single expense, measured against the week before.

Everything else you'd expect

Accounts and transfers

Bank, cash and your own pockets side by side, each with a running balance. Move money between them.

Credit cards

Limit, statement day and due day. See what's owed, what's left and how many days until payment.

Live gold and exchange rates

Gold coins, grams, silver, dollars or euros, valued at today's rate, with a total net worth.

Recurring transactions

Pick the days of the month once. Bills and salary post themselves.

14 languages, 16 currencies

Switch instantly, no restart. Currency is chosen separately from language. Full right-to-left support.

Light or dark

Pick one, or follow the system.

No account. No cloud. No bank login.

Mintor never asks who you are. Your transactions, accounts and debts are stored in a database on your phone, and we never receive them. The only thing the app downloads is a public list of gold and exchange rates. Mintor is free and shows a small banner ad; read exactly what that means in the privacy policy.
