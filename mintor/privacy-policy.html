Privacy Policy

Mintor: Budget & Expenses for iOS and Android
Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: Mintor has no accounts and no server of our own. Everything you enter (transactions, accounts, cards, debts, limits, holdings) is stored only on your device, and we, the developer, never receive it. The app downloads a public list of gold and exchange rates, and it shows a banner ad through Google AdMob. Google, not us, processes device and advertising data to deliver that ad. Your financial data is never given to Google or to anyone else.

1. Who we are

  • Developer and data controller: Emirhan Tunç, an individual developer in Türkiye ("we", "us").
  • Contact: emirhantunc7805@gmail.com
  • This policy online: https://emirhantunc.github.io/mintor/privacy-policy.html

This policy covers the Mintor app (the "App") as distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

2. What data is involved and where it goes

2.1 Data you enter: stays on your device

The App stores what you record in a database inside its private storage on your phone:

  • transactions (amount, category, account, date, title and optional description), recurring rules and transfers;
  • accounts, credit cards (name, limit, statement and due days), gold and currency holdings;
  • debts (the names you type for people, amounts, dates and payments);
  • your weekly limit, the notification inbox and your settings (language, currency, theme, month start day, alerts).

This data is not sent to us or to any server, and the App does not ask for a name, email address, phone number or bank login. We cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you, because we never have it.

Device backups. Your phone's operating system may include the App's data in backups that you control: Android's backup to your own Google account, and iCloud or computer backups on iPhone. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own terms; we have no access to them.

2.2 Gold and exchange rates

To show today's rates and value your gold and currency holdings, the App sends an anonymous request for a public rate file to latest.currency-api.pages.dev (the open-source "Exchange API" project, hosted on Cloudflare Pages). It does this when you open the App, at most every 15 minutes. The request contains none of your data. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and basic connection details to the host, which may keep them in short-lived logs. If you're offline, the App keeps showing the last rates it downloaded.

The App is free and shows a banner ad using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When your device is online, the SDK sends the following directly to Google, not to us:

2.4 Notifications

Budget alerts, the Monday weekly report and the evening reminder are local notifications, created on your phone from your own data. They don't go through any server, including ours. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in your phone's settings.

2.5 Feedback and reviews

  • Send Feedback opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to us. We only receive what you choose to send. We use it to answer you and improve the App.
  • The App may ask you to rate it, using Apple's or Google's built-in review prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple or Google; we only see what is published on the store.

2.6 What we don't do

No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, no in-app purchases, no social login, no access to your bank, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. We do not sell personal data.

2.7 Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Data in section 2.1 is processed only on your device to provide the App you chose to use.
  • Ads (section 2.3) and the rate request (section 2.2) are based on our legitimate interest in offering the App for free and showing current rates (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Where the law requires consent for personalized ads or for storing identifiers on your device, Google relies on that consent, and you can refuse or withdraw it (section 5).
  • Emails you send us are processed to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).

4. Device permissions

Android: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (rates and ads; the App works offline); POST_NOTIFICATIONS (alerts and reports, asked at runtime); RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and WAKE_LOCK (keep scheduled reminders and recurring transactions running after a restart); AD_ID and the Privacy Sandbox ad permissions, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

iOS: notification permission only. The App does not ask for tracking permission.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete data in the App: Settings → Delete a date range removes transactions between two dates. Settings → Delete all data removes all transactions, recurring rules, accounts, cards, holdings, debts, the weekly limit and notifications. Uninstalling the App removes everything.
  • Advertising: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads (or Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID; on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and Tracking. Data held by Google can be managed at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Use it offline: without a connection no ads are loaded and no rate request is made; everything else works.
  • Email us at emirhantunc7805@gmail.com with any request. Because we hold no personal data except emails you sent us, we will confirm that within 30 days and help you reach Google if needed.

6. Retention and security

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the App. It sits in the App's private storage, which other apps can't read under iOS and Android sandboxing. Protect it with your phone's screen lock.
  • With us: only emails you send, kept as long as needed to answer and at most 12 months.
  • With Google and the rate host: under their own retention policies (Google: policies.google.com/technologies/retention). All network traffic uses HTTPS.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Google.
