Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start adding records.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Everything you enter is stored in a database on your phone. It is never sent to us or to any server, so we can't see it. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

There is no cloud sync. On Android, your phone's own Google backup can restore the app's data when you set up a new device with the same Google account. On iPhone, an encrypted iCloud or computer backup of the whole phone carries the app's data to a new iPhone.

How do I make the month start on payday?

Settings → Month starts on, then pick a day from 1 to 31. Home and Transactions then show periods like "15 Oct – 14 Nov". If a month is shorter than the day you picked, the period starts on that month's last day.

How does the weekly limit work?

Set an amount in Settings → Weekly Limit. Mintor adds up your expenses for the current week and warns you at 80% and again if you go over. Turn the alert off with Budget Alert.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for Mintor in your phone's settings, and that Budget Alert and Weekly Report are on in Mintor's settings. Some Android phones also stop background work to save battery; set Mintor's battery use to "Unrestricted".

How do recurring transactions work?

When you add a transaction, turn on Recurring transaction and pick the days of the month. Mintor posts it on those days. If a day doesn't exist in a month (for example the 31st), it's posted on the last day of that month.

Where do gold and exchange rates come from? They look off.

Mintor downloads a free public rate list when you open the app, at most every 15 minutes. It's for an overview, not for trading, and can differ from your bank's or jeweller's buy/sell price. Without a connection, the last downloaded rates are used.

How do I change the language or currency?

Settings → App Language and Settings → App Currency. They are separate, so you can use English with lira, for example. Changes apply immediately.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete a date range removes transactions between two dates. Settings → Delete all data removes all transactions, accounts, cards, holdings, debts, limits and notifications; your language, currency and theme are kept. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything. This can't be undone.

Why are there ads?

Mintor is free, and a small banner ad pays for its development. Ads never see your financial data. See section 3 of the privacy policy.
