Pantry Parkour

A very small mouse. A very big house. One smell of cheese.

Dot runs on her own. You decide when she jumps: tap to hop, hold to leap, and kick off walls in mid-air. Thirty handcrafted levels across five rooms of the house (the kitchen counter, the pantry, the study, the laundry room and the cellar where the cat sleeps), all drawn like a risograph print.
