FAQ

How do I play?
Tap anywhere to start. Dot runs on her own: tap to jump, hold to jump higher. In mid-air, touch a wall and tap again to kick off it. Get her to the cheese at the end of the level.

Dot turned around!
Running into a wall or a step turns her around. Jump over small steps to keep going.

What are the matchboxes for?
Dot stops on a matchbox and waits for your next tap, so you can time a jump past a drip, a ruler or the cat's paw. She also sits still while riding a ruler; tap to hop off.

What are stamps and swift times?
Every level hides three stamps, and has a "swift" time to beat. Stamps unlock special hats in the wardrobe.

A level is too hard.
Thimbles are checkpoints. Every fall restarts Dot at the last thimble she touched, so take it one stretch at a time.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Not yet. Progress is stored only on your device.

How do I start over?
Settings (gear icon) → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm.
