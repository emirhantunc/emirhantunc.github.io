Privacy Policy — Puzzle Pix

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in Puzzle Pix (formerly TileDrift; the "App"), a single-player sliding puzzle game for Android.

  • Controller / developer: Emirhan Tunç, an individual developer ("we", "us" or "our").
  • Contact for privacy questions and requests: emirhantunc7805@gmail.com
  • Web version of this policy: https://emirhantunc.github.io/puzzle-pix/privacy-policy.html

This policy applies to the App as distributed on Google Play. It does not cover third-party websites or services you reach from an advertisement; those have their own policies.

In short: the App has no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK of our own and no server of our own. We, the developer, receive no personal data about you. Your progress and any photos you add stay on your phone. The App is free because it shows advertisements through Google AdMob, and it is Google, not us, that processes device and advertising data to deliver those ads.

2. Information we collect and how it is used

2.1 Information you provide directly

None that leaves your phone. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or messages. There is no registration, login, chat or online content.

2.2 Photos you choose (optional)

You can turn your own pictures into puzzles. When you tap to add a photo, Android's picker opens and you choose a single picture. The App never browses or reads your gallery on its own.

  • The App saves a square, resized copy of the chosen picture in its private storage on your phone so you can play it again later.
  • The photo and the copy are never uploaded, sent to us, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.
  • You can delete a saved photo inside the App at any time. The original in your gallery is not affected.

2.3 Information stored only on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • levels completed, stars, experience points, hints, daily-puzzle streak, missions and achievements;
  • your settings (language, sound, vibration, tile numbers);
  • the photos you added (section 2.2);
  • counters used to space out advertisements (for example, how many puzzles you have solved).

This data never leaves your phone, is not sent to us or anyone else, and contains no identifiers. It is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it (section 5).

The share button on the win screen only copies a short text result (for example "Puzzle Pix · Level 12 · ★★★") to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): personalized advertising and storing/reading identifiers on your device for that purpose. You are asked through Google's consent message before any personalized ads are served.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): showing non-personalized (contextual) ads to fund a free game, limiting how often ads appear, and detecting fraud and abuse.
  • Game data and photos (sections 2.2–2.3) are processed on your phone only, to provide the game you asked to play.

5. Deleting your data

Because the App has no accounts, there is no account to delete and we hold no personal data on any server. You stay in control:

Delete your game data and photos

  • To remove a single photo: open Free, select the photo and tap the trash icon.
  • To erase everything (progress, settings and saved photos): Android Settings → Apps → Puzzle Pix → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App.

Delete or reset advertising data

  • Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads): Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID; manage Ads privacy (Privacy Sandbox).
  • Change your ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): clear the App's storage as above; Google's consent message is shown again the next time you open the App.
  • Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's policies; review and delete Google-held data at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy or request deletion at https://support.google.com/policies/troubleshooter/9009584.

Request by email
Email emirhantunc7805@gmail.com with the subject "Data request – Puzzle Pix". Because we store no personal data, we will confirm that within 30 days and explain how to exercise your rights with Google if needed. We will never ask for passwords.

6. Data retention and security

  • On-device game data and photos: kept until you delete them, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
  • Data held by us: none. We do not operate servers or databases for this App. If you email us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to answer you and no longer than 12 months.
  • Data processed by Google: retained according to Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Security: game data and photos are stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Data sent by the ads SDK uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (COPPA) or, in the EEA/UK, from children under the age of digital consent in their country without parental consent. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App in a way that caused personal data to be collected, contact emirhantunc7805@gmail.com. We will help you delete the local data and request deletion of advertising data from Google.

8. Your rights

8.1 Everyone

You can play offline (no ads, no data sent), delete the App's data at any time (section 5), and reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

8.2 EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR / FADP)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are fulfilled by Google as the ad provider; we will help you direct them. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8.3 United States (CCPA/CPRA and other state laws)

The App enables the categories of data in section 2.4 (identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, device information) to be collected by Google for advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Where using advertising identifiers for personalized ads counts as "selling" or "sharing", you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID in Android settings. You also have the rights to know/access, delete and correct, and to non-discrimination. Send requests to emirhantunc7805@gmail.com; we will respond within 45 days.

8.4 Türkiye (KVKK)

Residents of Türkiye have the rights set out in Article 11 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, including learning whether data is processed and requesting its deletion. Contact us at the email above.

9. International transfers

Google and Unity may process data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA/UK/Switzerland.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change (for example, if in-app purchases or analytics are added), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect, and update the Google Play disclosures accordingly.
