FAQ

How do I play?
Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it in. Keep sliding until the picture is complete.

How do I get three stars?
Each level shows a move target. Finish within it for three stars.

I'm stuck.
Tap the light bulb for a hint, the eye to peek at the finished picture, or # to show tile numbers.

How do I play with my own photo?
Open Free and tap Add Your Photo. The game keeps a cropped copy only on your phone. To remove it, select it in Free and tap the trash icon; the original in your gallery is not affected.

I used to play TileDrift.
Puzzle Pix is the new version of TileDrift. Progress from the old version doesn't carry over, sorry.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Not yet. Progress is stored only on your phone.

How do I start over?
Android Settings → Apps → Puzzle Pix → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes your progress and your saved photos.
