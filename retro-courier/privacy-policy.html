1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in Retro Courier (also listed as "Retro Courier: Pixel Delivery"; the "App"), a single-player delivery game for Android and iOS.

Controller / developer: Emirhan Tunç, an individual developer ("we", "us" or "our").
Contact for privacy questions and requests: emirhantunc7805@gmail.com
Web version of this policy: https://emirhantunc.github.io/retro-courier/privacy-policy.html

This policy applies to the App as distributed on Google Play and the Apple App Store. It does not cover third-party websites or services you reach from an advertisement; those have their own policies.

In short: the App has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no server of our own. We, the developer, receive no personal data about you. Your progress stays on your device. The App is free because it shows advertisements through Google AdMob; Google, not us, processes device and advertising data to deliver those ads. If you buy Remove Ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple.

2. Information we collect and how it is used

2.1 Information you provide directly

None that leaves your device. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or messages. There is no registration, login, chat or online content. Names you type in the game (for example, a name for your child in the story) are stored only on your device.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves a file in its private storage on your device containing:

  • your game progress: day and time, money, receipts, taxes, deliveries, motos, furniture and room layouts, story progress and relationship values;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration);
  • whether you bought Remove Ads;
  • counters used to space out advertisements (for example, when the last ad was shown).

This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and contains no identifiers. It is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it (section 5).

2.3 In-app purchase

The App offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads, processed by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS) through Unity In-App Purchasing. Google or Apple handle your payment details; we never see your card, name or email. The App receives only a purchase confirmation (receipt) from the store to unlock the feature, and keeps a "purchased" flag on your device. Google and Apple may share sales reports with us that do not identify you.

On iOS, the App may ask for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not available to the ads SDK and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

2.6 Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): personalized advertising and storing/reading identifiers on your device for that purpose. You are asked through Google's consent message before any personalized ads are served.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): showing non-personalized (contextual) ads to fund a free game, limiting how often ads appear, and detecting fraud and abuse.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): verifying and restoring a Remove Ads purchase you made.
  • Game data (section 2.2) is processed on your device only, to provide the game you asked to play.

5. Deleting your data and changing your choices

Because the App has no accounts, there is no account to delete and we hold no personal data on any server. You stay in control:

Delete your game data

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Retro Courier → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • iOS: delete the App from your device.

A Remove Ads purchase stays linked to your store account and can be restored after reinstalling (Settings → Restore purchases).

Delete or reset advertising data

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads): Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID; manage Ads privacy (Privacy Sandbox).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Change your ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): clear the App's storage or reinstall it; Google's consent message is shown again the next time you open the App.
  • Data already collected by Google is handled under Google's policies; review and delete Google-held data at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy or request deletion at support.google.com/policies/troubleshooter/9009584.

Request by email

Email emirhantunc7805@gmail.com with the subject "Data request – Retro Courier". Because we store no personal data, we will confirm that within 30 days and explain how to exercise your rights with Google, Apple or Unity if needed. We will never ask for passwords.

6. Data retention and security

  • On-device game data: kept until you delete it, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
  • Data held by us: none. We do not operate servers or databases for this App. If you email us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to answer you and no longer than 12 months.
  • Data processed by Google, Apple and Unity: retained according to their policies (for Google: policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Security: game data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Data sent by the ads SDK and the stores uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (COPPA) or, in the EEA/UK, from children under the age of digital consent in their country without parental consent. Ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App in a way that caused personal data to be collected, contact emirhantunc7805@gmail.com. We will help you delete the local data and request deletion of advertising data from Google.

8. Your rights

8.1 Everyone

You can play offline (no ads, no data sent), delete the App's data at any time (section 5), and reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

8.2 EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR / FADP)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests are fulfilled by Google as the ad provider (or by Google Play / Apple for purchases); we will help you direct them. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8.3 United States (CCPA/CPRA and other state laws)

The App enables the categories of data in section 2.4 (identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, device information) to be collected by Google for advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Where using advertising identifiers for personalized ads counts as "selling" or "sharing", you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID in Android settings or choosing Ask App Not to Track on iOS. You also have the rights to know/access, delete and correct, and to non-discrimination. Send requests to emirhantunc7805@gmail.com; we will respond within 45 days.

8.4 Türkiye (KVKK)

Residents of Türkiye have the rights set out in Article 11 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, including learning whether data is processed and requesting its deletion. Contact us at the email above.

9. International transfers

Google, Apple and Unity may process data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA/UK/Switzerland.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change (for example, if accounts, cloud saves or analytics are added), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect, and update the store privacy disclosures accordingly.
