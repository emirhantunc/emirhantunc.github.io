Questions

How do I play?

Use the joystick to walk or ride and the round button to act. Walk to your moto and press Ride. Orders come in on your pager: ride to the restaurant, walk in and take the order, then deliver it to the address before the time runs out. Knock on doors to go inside.

What are receipts and the accountant for?

Everything you buy gives you a receipt, which goes into your shoebox. Once a week, visit Signora Ferri at Studio Ferri on Piazza del Tram and sort each receipt into work, private or bin. Work costs lower your tax, so sort carefully.

Giulia left me. What happened?

Giulia needs your time and your half of the rent. Spend evenings with her after 18:00 and give her the rent share each week. Your phone shows her mood and when the rent is due.

I ran out of petrol.

Fill up at a petrol station. If you're stuck with an empty tank, you can watch a short video for a free can of petrol. Watching it is always your choice.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and tap Remove ads. It's a one-time purchase that turns off the ads shown between days. Optional petrol videos stay available in case you want them.

I bought Remove Ads but ads are back (new phone or reinstall).

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google or Apple account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email us with your order number (Google Play: the GPA.… number in your receipt email; App Store: the order ID in your Apple receipt).

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store. Request a refund through Google Play or Apple's Report a Problem.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not yet. Your progress is saved only on your phone, with no account and no cloud save.

I played the old version of Retro Courier.

Version 2.0 is a brand-new game, rebuilt from scratch with a new town and new art. Progress from the old version does not carry over, sorry.

How do I start over?

Android: Settings → Apps → Retro Courier → Storage → Clear storage. iPhone/iPad: delete the app and install it again. This erases your progress for good. A Remove Ads purchase can be restored afterwards.

Does the game need the internet?

No. You can play offline. The internet is only used to load ads and to buy or restore Remove Ads.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open Settings in the game and tap Daily reminder. You can also turn notifications off in your phone's settings. The reminder comes at most once a day, stops after a week if you don't play, and never contains ads.
