Snowplow Village

Every night the storm buries Kestrel Hollow. Every morning, the village needs its roads.

Take the wheel of Grandpa Arlo's old snowplow. Drag to steer, push the snow aside and dig a road from the town square to every house before the fuel runs out or the sun comes up. Cross frozen rivers, slide on black ice, outrun avalanches and brave the blizzard, and find out where Arlo went.
