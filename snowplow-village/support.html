FAQ

How do I play?
Hold anywhere and drag: the plow drives toward your finger and pushes the snow aside. A house's windows light up once a clear road connects it to the town square. Connect every house at the same time to win the night.

Why did a house go dark again?
Snow keeps falling, and avalanches can bury roads. Plow that road again, or plan a route that finishes before the old roads fill in.

How do I earn more stars?
Stars depend on the fuel left in the tank when the village is dug out. Shorter routes and roads that branch to several houses save fuel. Upgrades in Arlo's Garage help too.

What does the Salt Spreader do?
Tap the salt button during a night: the snow around the plow melts and stops settling for a while.

How do I get more notes?
Villagers leave a note after many of the nights. Read them in Notes on the village screen.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Not yet. Progress is stored only on your device.

How do I start over?
Settings (gear icon) → Reset progress.
